Acoustic Bureau

Bringing you the tools to meet legal noise limits, building codes and environmental regulations.

Our Motto

We collaborate closely with design teams, developers and regulators to incorporate acoustics early in the planning process — potentially saving you some costly post construction rectification works.

We pride ourselves in finding tailored, technically sound solutions.

We produce clear reports, modeling outputs, and recommendations that are easy to understand and implement.

Our Skills

Environmental Planning

What we bring to the table:

  • Baseline noise and vibration monitoring
  • Predictive modelling of future noise impacts
  • High-quality technical reports for planning applications

Our deliverables ensure developments are legally compliant, community-friendly, and acoustically sustainable.

Industrial Acoustics

We support you:

  • Clear compliance strategy
  • Practical cost effective noise control
  • Environmental Management Plans

Stakeholder involvement and open discussions with the regulator enable a fast track approval process.

Transportation Noise

We offer:

  • Identification of noise receivers
  • Predictive noise modelling
  • Noise mapping
  • Noise monitoring

Our technical reports include monitoring and predictive modeling to support structure plans, subdivisions for a smooth process from planning to construction.

Commercial Fit-outs

Spaces sound as good as they look:

  • Speech privacy and acoustic separation
  • HVAC noise
  • Testing and commissioning
  • Room acoustics

Risk is controlled with a fine tuned approach that complies with your comfort goals (NCC, Greenstar, WELL).

Building Acoustics

Noise control for buildings:

  • Reverberation
  • Plant noise
  • Acoustic detailing and code compliance
  • Pre and post construction testing

We are here to take residential, educational, healthcare, hospitality and office projects to the next level.

Advisory

Judgement, experience and direction:

  • Strategic risk advice
  • Codes standards and policy development
  • Peer reviews
  • Community engagement

We help clients make good decisions about sound — decisions that affect comfort, compliance, cost, and community acceptance.

Make your projects buildable, approvable, and socially acceptable

While protecting communities from noise and vibration.

FAQs

What regulations apply to my project?

With 18 years experience we can assist you identify which regulations apply to your project (SPP5.4, NCC, Environmental Protection Noise Regulations 1997, Environmental Protection Act 1986 Part IV and Part V, EPBRC etc.)

What is the cost of hiring an acoustician?

Based on the project brief we can offer solutions and involvement that tune to your strategic needs and will be competitive.
